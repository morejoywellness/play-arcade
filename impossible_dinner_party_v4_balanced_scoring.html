Select any seat to begin inviting guests.
How to play: Seat exactly six guests, then serve dinner to see your score and the evening's gossip. Adjacent guests have the strongest influence on each other; guests opposite each other still interact. Every guest pairing has its own compatibility rating and character-specific explanation. Some rivalries are so intense that distance won't save you. Try rearranging the same six people to improve the evening. A score of 50 is an ordinary dinner; 80+ is exceptional, and 100 is genuinely rare.
